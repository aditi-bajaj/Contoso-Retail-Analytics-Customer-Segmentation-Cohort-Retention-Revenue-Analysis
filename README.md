# 📊 Contoso Retail Dataset Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This is an end-to-end data analytics project using **SQL and Power BI** to analyze customer behavior, retention patterns, and revenue trends based on the Contoso retail dataset.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Analyze customer purchasing behavior, retention trends, revenue concentration, and product performance to uncover actionable retail business insights using SQL and Power BI.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* SQL (PostgreSQL)
* Power BI
* DAX
* Excel (for initial data handling)

- Customer segmentation (overall and country-level)

- Pareto analysis and revenue concentration insights
- Customer purchase behavior analysis

- Retention rate and cohort impact analysis

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- A small percentage of customers contributed disproportionately to total revenue.
- Several customers purchased from only a single product category, indicating strong niche preferences.
- Certain cohorts demonstrated significantly stronger long-term retention.
- Product categories showed varying dependency on high-value customers.
- Monthly retention rates highlighted customer drop-off patterns after initial purchases.
  
📌 For **detailed Business Insights**, refer ➡️ `business_reports/business_insights/`
